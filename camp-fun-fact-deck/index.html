Fan-made replacement deck for family gameplay. Does not reproduce the original CAMP® Fun Fact cards and is not affiliated with Education Outdoors.

How to use it

When a player lands on the Camp Clubhouse, reveal one Fun Fact for everyone to hear. That completes the player’s turn, matching the printed rules.

  • Next Fun Fact advances the shuffled deck.
  • No fact repeats until all 99 have been shown.
  • Shuffle starts a fresh random order.
  • Read Aloud lets the phone read the fact.
  • Your place is saved on this device.
